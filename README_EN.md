# 🗣️ YellOut / YellZone

> A minimalist voice-driven emotional vent & sanctuary tailored for middle-aged professionals and overwhelmed adults. Choose your target, press to yell, and let the burden go.

[English](./README_EN.md) | [中文](./README.md)

---

## 💡 Background & Philosophy

The weight carried by middle-aged individuals often stems from the social expectation that they "must never break down." You cannot lash out at work, and you cannot burden your family at home.

**YellOut** provides an unconditionally safe, minimalist, and zero-social-pressure sanctuary:
- **Minimalist Flow**: Zero bloated social feeds. The homepage is your venting chamber.
- **Target Selection**: Precision venting tags (e.g., *Boss / Partner / Client / Life / Mortgage / Anonymous*).
- **Voice-First Catharsis**: Press and hold to shout or whisper. Real-time audio pitch-shifting protects your acoustic identity. Release to send.
- **Pure Empathy**: The community square only permits brief voice encouragements and non-verbal reaction gestures (*"Hug"*, *"I Get You"*, *"Silent Shoulder Tap"*). No toxic comment wars, no unsolicited preaching.

---

## 🛠️ Core Features

1. **Venting Chamber (Home)**
   - **Target Picker**: Quick-select recipient tags (*Boss*, *Partner*, *Client*, *Life*, etc., with custom tag support).
   - **Press & Yell**: Central pulsating soundwave button with real-time DSP pitch shifting (*Low Baritone, Robot, Ethereal*).
   - **Burn to Ash (🔥)**: Slide up while yelling to instantly incinerate the voice note into digital ashes with combustion sound and haptic feedback—destroying it on the spot with zero traces left behind.
   - **Car Window Night Rain (🌧️)**: Integrated Web Audio synthesized rain ambience recreating the solitary solace of sitting in a parked car late at night.

2. **Resonance Square (Community)**
   - **Minimalist Cards**: Displays only recipient tags, dynamic anonymous codenames, and pitch-shifted voice snippets (up to 60s).
   - **Zero-Burden Interaction**: Graphic reaction gestures (*Hug / I Get You / Resonate / Shoulder Tap*) and 10-second warm voice notes. No text input, no follower counts, no algorithmic pressure.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety Safeguards

- **Default Voice Pitch-Shifting**: Obfuscates acoustic biometric features to prevent recognition by acquaintances.
- **Absolute Anonymity**: No address book access, no social login requirements, auto-expiring 24-hour post lifecycle.
- **Crisis & High-Risk Detection**: Automated safety triggers detect severe despair and gently connect users to 24/7 psychological crisis support hotlines.
